About

I'm Rance, a 1st year student at Aklan State University – Kalibo Campus, taking BS Information Technology, major in Software Engineering. I'm working toward a career in offensive security: red teaming, penetration testing, vulnerability research and digital investigations.

I practice on TryHackMe, Hack The Box and picoCTF, and I build tools for the categories I compete in, mostly network forensics and cryptography. I'd rather understand how a system works deeply enough to break it and explain it than just run a scanner against it.

I lead the ASU Cyber Guild at the College of Computer Studies, and our team placed in the Top 5 at the HackForGov 5 Region VI qualifier. Next objective: HackForGov 2027.
